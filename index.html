A little sparkle in every kitchen, every celebration

Scintillicious Sweets N More started with one oven, one big love for sugar and a belief that every celebration deserves food people talk about for weeks. Today we are a Lagos restaurant, bakery and event team under one roof.

We bake our cakes and pastries fresh, cook our small chops and party trays from scratch, and plan events down to the last napkin, so you can enjoy your own party.
